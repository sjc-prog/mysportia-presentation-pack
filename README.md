## Two worlds — do not mix them

This repo serves two audiences with **different numbers**:

- **BOI submission** (the current job): 20 pilot venues · 15%/85% built · licence-fee model · company from 17 Sep 2025 · 5.65M THB investment
- **Investor pitch** (the Figma source of `09-figma-final`): 34 venues · 162M THB GTV · 5% per transaction · 2021 timeline

**Reuse the Figma visuals. Never the Figma numbers.** `04-FACTS.md` is authoritative for anything BOI.
