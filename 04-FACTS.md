# 🚫 Words that must never appear. Not once. Anywhere.

```
Exsportia · Israel · Bulgaria · any foreign entity · any prior owner ·
IP transfer · assignment · licence acquisition ·
commission · take rate · 5% · transaction fee (as the business model) ·
free · no subscription ·
MVP · MVP complete · fully developed · end-to-end complete ·
enhance · improve · optimise · expand · refine · modernise · further develop ·
migrate existing · integrate existing ·
school · international school · Tree Yard · Ipingi
```

Two of these look harmless and are not:

**"Commission", "take rate", "5%".** BOI's own rule for this activity — Por.12/2566 clause 5.2.1 —
**excludes commission and intermediary income from qualifying revenue.** Putting it on a slide argues
the applicant out of the tax exemption it is applying for.

**"Improve", "expand", "modernise", "further develop", "enhance".** Software **development** carries
the corporate income tax exemption. Software **modification** does not. Every word implying we are
improving something that already exists costs money. This applies to captions, chart labels and
alt text as much as to body copy.

# ✅ Words to use

```
MySportia · develop · build · create · design · new · in Thailand ·
by our Thai engineering team · pilot venues · software licence fee
```

## The Thailand dividend — approved argument

Connected revenue → automatic accounting → direct filing to the Revenue Department (**built under
this project**) → a bigger, cleaner tax base at no cost to the state. Plus: the marketplace brings
venues new customers and the tools to grow, so the base grows as well as cleans. **Never** claim a
specific uncollected-tax figure, and never phrase it as "Thailand is missing most of the money" —
the mechanism is the argument, not an invented number.
