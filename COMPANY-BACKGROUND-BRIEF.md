# BRIEF 4 — Company Background

> **Job:** rebuild the BOI submission document *"Company Background"* (10.2 row 3).
> **Format:** A4 **landscape**, 4–5 pages, PDF under 5MB.
> Read `DECK-BRIEF.md` first for the brand system — it applies here unchanged.
>
> ⚠️ **Set the page size to landscape at export.** The last three documents came back as landscape artwork on portrait pages with no rotation flag, so every page opened sideways and had to be repaired.

---

## 1 · What this document has to do

This is the company's identity card for a government officer. They are checking one thing above all: **is this a real Thai company, properly constituted, with its facts in order?**

Every line in the current version is already correct and evidenced. Nothing needs rewriting — the entire job is presentation. Today it reads like a filing; it should read like a **company record**: precise, structured, quietly authoritative.

**The feeling: a passport, not a poster.** Restraint is the design. Where the deck is expressive, this is exact.

---

## 2 · The structural change

Five dense blocks currently share two pages. Give the important ones room:

### Page 3 — What the company does, and the timeline
The purpose paragraph, set generously — it is the clearest statement of the company's mission anywhere in the pack:

> *"The company develops MySportia: software for sports and activity venues, together with a marketplace that connects players to those venues — national infrastructure built to raise participation in sport and activity, including amateur and minority sports, by automating the business side so venues can concentrate on delivering the service."*

Then two short lines: no trade in the first accounting period; built and tested at pilot venues; trading from 1 September 2026.

Beneath, the **timeline as a horizontal rail** (same idiom as the Track Record exhibit — coloured dots, evidence beneath each stop): 17 Sep 2025 · 17 Sep–31 Dec 2025 · Through Aug 2026 · **20 Aug 2026** · 1 Sep 2026 · Sep 2026. Make 20 Aug 2026 the dominant stop.

### Page 4 — Product status and finances
**The 15 / 85 split, as the page's centrepiece.** One bar or two blocks: 15% built and running, 85% to be developed under this application. This is the single most consequential fact in the whole submission — give it space and let nothing compete with it.

Supporting text: approximately 15% exists today and runs at **20 pilot venues** — venue management back office, marketplace listing and booking (live at mysportia.com), and payment infrastructure, where **the company operates as a master merchant with Stripe and Omise** (PromptPay, Thai QR, cards). The remaining ~85% is eight new functions, none of which exists today, to be developed in Thailand over twelve months.

### Page 5 — Employment
The company has **no employees today**. Every role is new employment created after approval — that framing matters to BOI and should be stated as a headline, not buried.

Visualise the growth: **4 → 8 → 12** Thai engineers across three years, plus five foreign leadership roles. Then the compliance line: **Thai IT salaries of 2,080,000 THB in year one, against the 1,500,000 THB condition** — show the margin, it is a strength.

## 4 · Craft notes

- **Tabular figures throughout.** Registration numbers, share counts and money must align in columns. This document's authority comes from looking exact.
- **Thai script must be set correctly** — บริษัท เอ็ดดูเคชั่น เอไอ กรุ๊ป จำกัด and บอจ.5. Use a font with proper Thai support; broken Thai glyphs would undermine the whole document.
- **One accent per page**, pink: 51% on page 2, the 20 Aug pivot on page 3, the 15/85 split on page 4, the salary margin on page 5.
- **No photography, no product screenshots.** This is a corporate record. Its only imagery is structure — panels, rails, and the shareholding figure.
- **Footer every page:** thin gradient rule, `Education AI Group Co., Ltd.` left, page number right.
- Closing line, small: *"Every statement in this document is evidenced by the corporate documents and audited financial statements attached to the application."*
