# BRIEF 3 — Track Record & Operational Proof

> **Job:** rebuild the BOI submission document *"Records and details of past businesses"*.
> **Format:** A4 **landscape**, 4–5 pages, PDF under 5MB.
> Read `DECK-BRIEF.md` first for the brand system — it applies here unchanged.
>
> ⚠️ **Export landscape pages properly.** The previous document was exported as landscape artwork on portrait pages with no rotation flag, so every page opened sideways and had to be fixed afterwards. Set the page size to landscape at export.

---

## 1 · What this document has to do

BOI asks for *"records and details of past businesses"*. The officer is answering one question: **has this company, and the person behind it, actually done anything — or is this a plan on paper?**

The current version answers it correctly but reads like a memo: two dense pages of tables and paragraphs. Everything in it is true and evidenced. The job is to make that evidence **land**.

**The feeling to create: a receipt, not a pitch.** Every claim sits next to its proof. Dates, documents, live systems. Quiet authority.

---

## 2 · The structural change

The current document has three sections crammed onto two pages. Give each its own space:

| Page | Section |
|---|---|
| **1 · Cover** | Title and one-line premise |
| **2 · The company's operating record** | The timeline from incorporation to trading — as a visual rail, not a table |
| **3 · What operates daily** | The four operating systems, each with a real screenshot |
| **4 · The founder's business record** | Justin's twenty-year history as a horizontal timeline |
| **5 · Evidence index** *(optional)* | Every claim mapped to the document that proves it |

## 4 · Craft notes

- **Dates are structural.** Set them in a mono or tabular figure style so the timeline reads as a record, not prose.
- **One accent per page.** Pink for the pivot moment (20 Aug 2026) and the 800,000+ figure. Nothing else competes.
- **Evidence column in warm grey**, smaller — present but subordinate. It should feel like a footnote that happens to be load-bearing.
- **No stock photography.** Only real screenshots and real marks. This document's currency is authenticity.
- **Footer every page:** thin gradient rule, `Education AI Group Co., Ltd.` left, page number right.

---

## 5 · Hard rules

- **20 pilot venues.** Never any other figure.
- Banned: `Exsportia` · `Israel` · `MVP` · `enhance` / `improve` / `optimise` / `expand` / `refine`
- Dates are locked: incorporated **17 September 2025** · production **20 August 2026** · trading from **1 September 2026**
- **Bitch Events Ltd is the real registered company name** — keep it exactly; do not soften it
- Do not add any claim that is not in this brief. Every line here is already evidenced
- ⚠️ `assets/09-figma-final` carries investor-world numbers (34 venues, 162M GTV). **Composition yes, numbers never.**
