## What the deck is actually arguing

Three in four people stop playing sport after they leave education — not from lack of interest, from
lack of access. The obvious build was a consumer marketplace, and it could not be built: **the venues
were the bottleneck.** You cannot build a marketplace on top of businesses that are not bookable.
Hotels got booking systems before Booking.com was possible.

So the order was inverted. **First fix the venues, then build the thing people actually use.**

- **Phase 1 — fix the venues.** Built.
- **Phase 2 — the marketplace.** Built.
- **Phase 3 — the lifestyle layer.** *This project.* Profiles and skill ratings, opponent matching, leagues, tournaments and national rankings, community, native mobile.

That is why the 15/85 split is what it is. **The 15% is the plumbing. The 85% is the product.**
Slides 9 and 13 carry that argument and they are the deck.
